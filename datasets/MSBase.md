# MSBase Registry

[Back to overview](../README.md) · Registry website: [msbase.org](https://www.msbase.org/)

International observational MS registry and platform for collaborative outcomes research, collecting longitudinal
clinical follow-up from MS centres worldwide [[1]](#r1).

## Data

| Dataset | People with MS | Hospital visits | Countries | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Initial extract (previous work) | 109,283 | 1,308,607 | 45 | [[2]](#r2) |
| After curation (RRMS and SPMS visits) | 56,578 | 542,632 | 37 | [[2]](#r2) |

Curation retained visits with EDSS, age at visit, sex and a recorded diagnosis of RRMS or SPMS [[2]](#r2).

## Role in this project

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Use | External RRMS/SPMS evaluation only, reported by country |
| Not used for | Training, fine-tuning, self-supervised pretraining or country-fold training |
| CDW evaluation | Requires additional permission, which is being sought |
| PPMS | Not used |
| Previous cross-country training results | Reported as background only [[2]](#r2) |

## Registration and ethics

Registered with the WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ACTRN12605000455662). Approved by the
Melbourne Health Human Research Ethics Committee and site institutional review boards, unless exemptions were
granted under local regulations. Written informed consent was obtained from enrolled patients.
