# MS Clinical and Imaging Data Resource (MS-CIDR)

[Back to overview](../README.md) · Resource website: [International Progressive MS Alliance: MS Clinical and Imaging Data Resource](https://www.progressivemsalliance.org/ms-clinical-and-imaging-data-resource/)

Resource of the International Progressive MS Alliance providing anonymised longitudinal clinical follow-up and
conventional MRI from large MS clinical trials [[1]](#r1).

## How to read these figures

| Point | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Scope of counts | Totals describe the whole resource as reported by the Alliance (April 2026 data access guidelines). The dataset approved for a project may be a subset. |
| MRI scans | Counted per imaging time point, not per participant or per image sequence |
| Subtype | Classification at trial entry; not an observed RRMS-to-SPMS transition label at later visits |

## Planned variables

| Modality | Variables (where available) |
|---|---|
| Clinical | Demographics, disease duration, attack history, EDSS and functional systems, Timed 25-Foot Walk, 9-Hole Peg Test, PASAT or SDMT, comorbidities, visit trajectories |
| MRI | T1 pre and post gadolinium, T2, PD, FLAIR, MPRAGE or 3D FLASH, with associated metadata |

Trial, site and scanner information are used for stratified validation and sensitivity analyses, not as primary predictors.

## Role in this project

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Aims 1 and 2 | External clinical test |
| Aims 3 and 4 | MRI and clinical-MRI fusion development; Aim 4 uses the MS-CIDR partitions from Aim 3 |
| PPMS | Self-supervised pretraining only |

## Data handling

Only anonymised data provided through the Alliance are used. Data are stored on access-restricted university
infrastructure, limited to named users under the Data Access Agreement, are not redistributed and are not linked
to external individual-level datasets.
