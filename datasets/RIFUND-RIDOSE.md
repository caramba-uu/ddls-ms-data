# RIFUND-MS and RIDOSE-MS

[Back to overview](../README.md)

Swedish phase 3 randomised clinical trials of rituximab in relapsing MS, with longitudinal clinical follow-up.

## Studies

| Study | Design | Registration | Publication | Ethics (Sweden) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RIFUND-MS | Multicentre, rater-blinded, active-comparator phase 3 trial of rituximab versus dimethyl fumarate in RRMS or clinically isolated syndrome, at 17 Swedish hospitals [[1]](#r1) | [NCT02746744](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02746744) | Svenningsson et al. 2022 [[1]](#r1) | 2016/473-32 |
| RIDOSE-MS | Randomised phase 3 trial comparing rituximab 500 mg every 6 months with every 12 months in RRMS; sponsor Karolinska Institutet [[2]](#r2) | [NCT03979456](https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03979456) (EudraCT 2018-000721-31) |  | 2018/1061-31; 2026-00482-02 |

## Data

| Participants | Visits | Study period | Subtypes |
|---:|---:|---|---|
| ~325 (combined) | ~2,200 (combined) | Six years | RRMS; CIS in RIFUND-MS; no SPMS |

## Role in this project

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Aims 1 and 2 | External clinical evaluation; CDW evaluation where event counts permit |
| Aims 3 and 4 | Reused for clinical pretraining only after the Aim 1 and 2 evaluations are complete |
| Overlap with SMSReg | Linked participants are excluded from [SMSReg](SMSReg.md) development for Aims 1 and 2 |
